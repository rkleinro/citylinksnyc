How it works

No clubhouse, no dues, no single course. Just NYC golfers helping each other play more.

01 · POST A GAME

Share your tee time

Have a tee time? Post it. The whole network sees it. No tee time yet? Post anyway. Use it to find a group.

02 · JOIN A GAME

Tap "I'm in"

Browse the board, see who's playing where, and tap "I'm in" on anything that fits. The host gets your info and reaches out to confirm.

03 · STAY IN THE LOOP

Know when games go up

Get an email the moment someone posts a new game. A Sunday nudge to post yours. Turn either off from your profile, any time.
